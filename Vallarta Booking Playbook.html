Booking calendar

    Buy the flights recommended

    Air Canada's extra 20% off Mexico runs to Oct 19. WestJet has a winter Mexico sale with no blackout dates that includes Puerto Vallarta. Compare all three and book the best Feb 9–16 total directly with the airline.

    Buy if the four of you come in at about $1,800 or less before bags. Air Canada and Porter refund any direct booking within 24 hours. WestJet does too when departure is 7 or more days away. Book, sleep on it, and cancel the next morning if the condo falls through.

  3. Oct 13 – Oct 31

    Lock the condo with free cancellation

    February is the busiest month for Vallarta rentals, and people book about 61 days ahead on average. The good 2-bedroom units in small new buildings go first. Note the free-cancellation deadline on the confirmation and in your calendar.

  4. by Wed Nov 4

    Its welcome bonus ends Nov 4. The card's yearly $399-plus-tax companion voucher can't be used on WestJet Vacations packages.

  5. Nov 24 – Tue Dec 1Black Friday to Travel Tuesday

    Backstop deadline for flights last call

    If you haven't bought by now, buy this week. Last year, normal February weeks qualified but the Family Day weekend was blacked out. Re-price what you hold; rebook only if the saving beats any change fee.

  6. Dec 26 – Dec 31

    Porter Boxing Week

    Last year it was up to 25% off base fares, with the best discounts on Tuesday to Thursday and Saturday travel. Only worth checking if you're on Porter.

  7. Jan 5 – Jan 9

    Re-shop the condo

    While free cancellation still applies, compare against new listings. Read the USF seaweed bulletin only if a Caribbean option is back on the table.

  8. Jan 12 – Jan 26

    Last-minute packages appear

    Rarely for family suites or 2-bedroom rooms. RedFlagDeals posters report good family rooms selling out. Don't plan around this.

Time of day and day of week: no reliable effect. Expedia's 2026 Canadian data names Monday as the cheapest booking day, but the US and UK editions name different days with 2 to 3% gaps, and Google says the booking day "matters little." What matters is the departure day: Tuesdays here beat Saturdays by $200 to $2,300. The only clock times that matter are sale deadlines: Porter and Air Canada end at 11:59 pm Eastern, WestJet at 11:59 pm Mountain.

Worth the splurge

Velas Vallarta Suites, Marina Vallarta, all-inclusive: about $577/night (US$4,129 for the week), 9.2 from 1,003 reviews. Every room is a suite with a kitchen, and there's a kids' pool and kids' club. For all-inclusive, this was the standout value. Rooms are slightly dated and the beach is pretty rather than great.

Villa La Estancia, Flamingos: about $836/night. Real 1 to 3 bedroom villas, kids' pool, calm beach. The luxury option if budget stretches.

Skip these

  • Vidanta, Grand Bliss, Mayan Palace owner rentals: cheap at first, then mandatory guest fees ($42/day or a $299 flat fee) and hours-long timeshare pitches.
  • Villa del Palmar Flamingos: aggressive timeshare sales.
  • Paradise Village: fun pools, but basic rooms and a timeshare resort.
  • Posada de Bucerías: blogs recommend it, but it's adults-only on Booking.
  • Hotel Cielo Rojo: in San Pancho, and the family suite has no kitchen.
  • Grand Matlali, Villa Varadero, Bel Air: mixed cleanliness scores or dated rooms.

If Vallarta falls through

Backup destinations

Huatulco: best beaches, costly flights

Santa Cruz bay is the calm, toddler-friendly beach. EOS Suites (about $244/night, 2BR, fenced pool with a shallow end and baby gates, but only 22 reviews) and Nabani Condominios (about $375, 9.4 from 133 reviews, a 9-minute walk to the beach) are the gems. Camino Real Zaashila's condos (about $280) put a kitchen on a private, sheltered beach. Flights are the problem: Feb 6–13 was about $4,500 for four. Lock flights first there.

Los Cabos: stay on Médano only

The San José del Cabo hotel-zone beach is not swimmable. Lumina at Marea (about $304, 2BR, 9.5, a 6-minute walk to Médano; ask for a high unit away from the noisy street) and Condo Paraiso (about $326, 9.6, quieter, 12-minute walk) are the picks. Avoid Villa del Palmar and Pueblo Bonito for timeshare pressure. Whale-watching catamarans run about US$75 to $130 per person.

HST, fees and protection

The fine print that moves the total

  • HST: flights to Mexico are zero-rated, so no 13% HST. In a package, the Mexican hotel and tours aren't taxed either. Only Canadian pieces (some agency service fees, domestic legs) can carry HST. You save almost nothing on tax by choosing one route over the other.
  • TICO protection only applies if you book through an Ontario-registered agency or tour operator, and it repays what you paid, not replacement travel. Booking flights direct with the airline gets the 24-hour refund but no TICO coverage.
  • Mexico's non-resident fee (in the airfare) is 983 pesos now. The 2027 budget proposes 1,334.80, with votes due by Oct 31. Buying in 2026 might lock the old rate. That's about $25 per paid seat and unconfirmed, so don't let it drive the timing.
  • Price-drop guarantees are mostly gone: Sunwing ended its guarantee in 2024 and WestJet Vacations has none. Air Canada Vacations has one only with its $99-per-person CareFree add-on, capped at $200 per person, and you must claim it by phone. This is another reason to book flights and condo separately.
  • Porter tip: Costco sells $500 Porter e-gift cards for $449.99, and they stack with Porter sales. Paying with a gift card can void your credit card's travel insurance, so buy insurance separately.
